# Real-user test kit (about 20 minutes)

The rubric's strongest evidence for Impact is "a real user trying it." One session with one person is enough to quote in the report.

## Who to ask

In order of strength: a caseworker or reentry program staff member; a woman who has been through reentry (only if she wants to, and with no real record details needed); a social work or law student who works with this group; a classmate as a last resort. Tell them it is a class project and an estimate tool, not legal advice.

## Before you start

- Open https://second-chance-pathway-planner.onrender.com about a minute early (free hosting wakes up slowly).
- Have a timer and this page open. Ask permission to quote them and to take a screenshot (no real names or records in the screenshot).
- **Never ask for her real record.** Give her one of the sample cases below, or let a caseworker use a made-up client.

## Tasks (time each one)

1. **Self-serve (8 min):** "Pretend you are Maria: Hartford, a drug sale felony (Class D) from 2014, two kids living with family, staying with family, wants food service work. Use 'I'm planning for myself' and get to your 30-day plan." Note the minutes to reach the plan and anywhere they got stuck.
2. **Caseworker (5 min):** "You are a caseworker. Use 'I'm helping a client' and the template to enter a client of your choice (made up), then read the results." Note whether the "Not mentioned yet" box helped.
3. **Practice (5 min):** "Practice the job interview and answer Dana twice." Ask whether the coach's "Try saying…" was useful.

## Questions to ask afterward

1. How do people find out about Clean Slate and their next steps today? How long does that usually take? (This is your "before" number.)
2. Was anything confusing or wrong?
3. Would you use this, or give it to a client? What would stop you?
4. Which part was most useful: the record check, the plan, or practice?
5. On a scale of 1–5, how likely would you be to recommend it?
6. One thing you would change.
